# Types of Cats

Cats can be grouped in several ways: biologically, by domestic breed, Thai native breeds, and by coat length.

---

## 1. Biological Classification (Family Felidae)

The cat family has about 40 species, split into two main subfamilies.

### Big Cats (Pantherinae)

| Species | Key Traits |
|---|---|
| Lion | Lives in prides; males have manes; found in Africa and India |
| Tiger | Largest cat species; striped; strong swimmer |
| Leopard | Highly adaptable; hauls prey into trees; black panthers are melanistic leopards |
| Jaguar | Extremely strong bite; lives in Central and South America |
| Snow Leopard | Lives at high altitude; long thick tail; cannot roar |
| Clouded Leopard | Cloud-like markings; long canines relative to body size; found in Thailand |

### Small Cats (Felinae)

Examples include the cheetah (fastest land animal), puma, lynx, leopard cat, fishing cat, Asian golden cat, and the **domestic cat** (*Felis catus*), domesticated roughly 9,000–10,000 years ago.

Most cannot roar, but they can purr.

## 2. Popular Domestic Breeds

The number of recognized breeds depends on the registry: CFA recognizes about 45 breeds, TICA 70+.

| Breed | Coat | Temperament | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Persian | Long | Calm, affectionate | Flat face; prone to breathing issues and tear staining; needs daily brushing |
| Exotic Shorthair | Short, dense | Calm | Persian-type face with easier coat care |
| British Shorthair | Short, dense | Easygoing, independent | Dislikes frequent handling; gains weight easily |
| Scottish Fold | Short/long | Gentle | Folded ears come from a gene affecting cartilage; risk of painful joints |
| Maine Coon | Long | Friendly, intelligent | Very large; risk of heart disease (HCM) |
| Ragdoll | Semi-long | Goes limp when held, people-oriented | Blue eyes; good with children |
| Bengal | Short | High-energy, playful | Wild-looking spots; needs lots of activity |
| Sphynx | Hairless | Very affectionate | Needs regular baths to remove skin oils; sensitive to cold and sun |
| American Shorthair | Short | Adaptable | Hardy and easy to keep |

## 3. Thai Cats

Ancient Thai cat manuscripts describe many auspicious cat types (most sources cite around 17). The best-known surviving ones are:

- **Wichien Maat (Siamese)** — Cream coat with dark brown points on face, ears, legs, and tail; blue eyes; ancestor of the modern Siamese; vocal and attached to owners
- **Korat (Si Sawat / Malet)** — Silver-blue coat with shimmering tips; green eyes likened to young rice; heart-shaped face; considered lucky and often given as wedding gifts
- **Suphalak (Thong Daeng)** — Solid copper-brown coat; golden-yellow eyes; similar to the Burmese
- **Khao Manee** — Pure white; blue, yellow, or odd-colored eyes (odd eyes are prized); hearing should be checked, as white blue-eyed cats are prone to deafness
- **Konja** — Solid black; amber eyes; believed to ward off danger

## 4. By Coat Length

| Type | Care |
|---|---|
| Shorthair | Easy care; brush 1–2 times per week |
| Longhair | Brush almost daily to prevent mats and hairballs |
| Hairless | Not hypoallergenic; requires skin care instead of coat care |

---

> **Note:** If you plan to get a flat-faced, fold-eared, or giant breed, choose a breeder that health-screens the parent cats, or consider adopting a rescue cat, which is often hardier.
